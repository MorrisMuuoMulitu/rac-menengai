# Menengai: a photographic club journal

The user asked for a more professional, enthusiastic, sleek and memorable UI and UX, using frontend-design. This is an upgrade to existing routes and journeys, not a new application architecture. Authored design decisions below interpret that request; they are not interview quotations.

## Brief

- Audience: prospective members, project collaborators and people following the club's work in Nakuru.
- Character: professional, enthusiastic, sleek; warmth comes from actual members and activity photographs.
- Journey: encounter the club identity → understand the club → explore a programme → plan a Thursday visit → browse recent activity → read the magazine → contact or join.
- Energy: expressive opening, quieter introduction, interactive project spread, welcoming meeting invitation, photographic journal, decisive close.
- Peak: “I can see the people I’ll meet and the work I can join.” Lives in the opening photographic composition and resolves in the first-visit section.
- Signature: select a service interest and see its real photograph, programme and destination in one editorial spread.
- Grammar: photographic club journal, natural document scroll, full route navigation, asymmetric photo spreads and a meeting invitation ending. No pinned blank travel, fabricated impact statistics, generated people, or autoplay video.
- Assets: current supplied logo, club mantra, 2026 event photographs, club magazine, locally hosted Manrope and Barlow Condensed. Logo proportions and colors remain intact.
- Primary action: Join us; first-visit links point to the existing membership section.
- Feeling curve: curiosity (oversized identity beside the people); recognition (plain introduction); agency (select a programme); welcome (clear Thursday details); confidence (dated photographic records); intimacy (club magazine); readiness (join/contact).
- Tell-someone sentence: “It’s the site where you can find a project and picture your first Thursday with Menengai.”
- No authored silence or artificially extended scroll.

## Hero layer contract

Paper background stays still. A cranberry photographic backing sits behind the main gallery. The gallery and foreground seedling photograph overlap but have different restrained scroll travel. Stable typography and controls stay readable. The foreground is a separate authentic photograph, not an extracted person or synthetic landscape. Mobile recomposes to a type block followed by the gallery; reduced motion removes all travel.

## Reference principles

charity: water (https://www.charitywater.org/): visible routes to action and photographic project evidence. Adapt the clarity of action, not its donation claims or statistics.
Patagonia (https://www.patagonia.com/home/): navigation distinguishes stories from action opportunities. Adapt that separation to event records, projects and joining.
Rotary (https://www.rotary.org/get-involved/our-clubs/rotaract-clubs): service, professional development and fellowship remain the content spine.
