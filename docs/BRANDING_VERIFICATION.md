# Branding and media verification

The original files named logo.jpg and logo-transparent.png are a photograph of joined hands. They do not establish an approved Rotary/Rotaract club lockup. During final verification, a separate masterbrand source appeared at `assets/images/branding/rac-menengai-masterbrand-source.jpeg`, together with header/footer/favicon derivatives. Those workspace additions are preserved. Their origin and club approval were not established by this audit; do not treat the rendered mark as newly verified. No emblem was generated by this implementation workflow.

Rotary’s official Brand Center was consulted. Its base URL returned a redirect loop through the external fetch tool; its indexed official logo-library page lists Rotaract Masterbrand signatures in cranberry: https://brandcenter.rotary.org/en-us/search?dt_assettype=Logos&tab=0 . Obtain an approved club-specific masterbrand file from an authorised officer/Brand Center before signing off the current derived identity. Preserve proportions and clear space from its current official guidelines.

All original club assets remain in `assets/images/menengai/`. Unrelated Nairobi Central assets are retained as historical repository material and are not used in the redesigned site. New image files are compressed derivatives of existing assets; none are newly scraped or generated.
