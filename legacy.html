Who We Are

Nakuru’s home of young leaders in action

Chartered by the Rotary Club of Nakuru and part of Rotary International’s global Rotaract family, the Rotaract Club of Menengai has spent a decade turning energy into impact — from cleaning our rivers to welcoming new mothers at Bondeni Maternity.

Rooted in “Service Above Self,” we bring together young professionals and students aged 18–30 across District 9212 to lead with empathy, skill and joy.

Signature Project

Adopt-a-River: restoring Nakuru’s waterways

Our flagship environmental project tackles pollution at the source. In Barut, we installed a water filtration set, mobilized the community, cleaned River Njoro at Mwariki Bridge, and set up waste-disposal points to keep it clean.

  • River Njoro & Ndarugu cleanups with 20+ youth volunteers
  • Community sensitization on single-use plastic
  • Water filtration system installed for residents of Barut
  • Waste-disposal containers for lasting cleanliness
